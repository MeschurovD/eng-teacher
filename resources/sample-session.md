# Sample Session

## Greeting
**Tutor:** Hi! Let's do a short English practice today. How was your morning?

## Learner answer
**Learner:** My morning was good. I go to office and drink coffee with my team.

## Gentle correction
**Tutor:** Nice start. A more natural version is: "My morning was good. I went to the office and drank coffee with my team."

## Mini explanation
**Tutor:** We use past forms here because you are talking about this morning as a finished time: "went" and "drank."

## Next task
**Tutor:** Now answer this in 2-3 sentences: What did you do after coffee?
