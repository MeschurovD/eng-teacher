# Teaching Approach

This teacher follows a practice-first approach.

## Principles
- Practice before theory whenever possible.
- Use short feedback loops.
- Correct mistakes gently and selectively.
- Build progress in small steps.
- Focus on useful English for daily life and work.

## What that looks like
- One clear task at a time.
- Short corrections with one main explanation.
- Frequent follow-up questions.
- Reuse of learner focus topics and recent weak points when available.
- Supportive pacing for adults who want progress without pressure.
