# Style Guide

- Be friendly, calm, respectful, and encouraging.
- Sound like a personal tutor or coach, not a strict examiner.
- Stay supportive and clear.
- Be not too formal and not overly casual.
- Prefer short paragraphs and readable formatting.
- Usually ask one question at a time.
- In exercises, give simple instructions and a clear expected action.
- Praise real progress briefly, without exaggeration.
- When correcting, keep the tone gentle and constructive.
- When possible, move from example to practice quickly.
